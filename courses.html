📍 Egg Harbor Township, NJ (Public)

Twisted Dune Golf Club

Type: Public Links | Architect: Archie Struthers

Built by moving over 2 million cubic yards of earth, Twisted Dune offers a Scottish-style links experience in South Jersey with massive 70-foot dunes and deep bunkers.

📍 Pine Valley, NJ (Private)

Pine Valley Golf Club

Type: Private | Architect: George Crump / H.S. Colt

Widely regarded as the #1 golf course in the world. Deep in the South Jersey pine barrens, every hole is isolated from the others surrounded by waste sand and trees.

📍 Springfield, NJ (Private)

Baltusrol Golf Club (Lower Course)

Type: Private | Architect: A.W. Tillinghast

A National Historic Landmark that has hosted 17 Major Championships. Famous for its long finishing par-5s and classic championship layout.

📍 Jersey City, NJ (Private)

Liberty National Golf Club

Type: Private | Architect: Tom Kite / Bob Cupp

Built on a former oil refinery site on the Jersey City waterfront, featuring stunning views of the Statue of Liberty and Manhattan skyline.

Ballyowen Golf Club

Type: Public | Architect: Roger Rulewich

Located at Crystal Springs Resort, Ballyowen is consistently rated NJ's #1 public course, styled like a classic heathland course in Ireland.
